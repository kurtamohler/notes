
How did you learn to speak? When you were a child, you learned most of your words by observing how other people interact with the physical world and hearing the words they said. You drew correlations between the sounds that people's voices made and the physical objects or processes that you observed along with those sounds. For instance, maybe your parents would held a ball toward you and said "ball", or they pointed their finger at a car and said "car". Maybe you performed some kind of action, like smiling or crying, and you heard your parents say the word for it. Maybe your parents heard your successful attempts and then smiled and cheered you on, confirming and strengthening the correlation that you made.

At some point you started to realize that a word isn't just correlated but in fact stands as a symbol specifically to represent and communicate about a thing. Like a finger pointing to a cloud in the sky, the word "cloud" also points to the cloud. You also learned that there are words to represent physical qualities of things. Maybe you unsuccessfully tried to move a table and someone told you it was too "heavy". Maybe you pet a cat and someone told you that its fur is "soft".

You learned many of the words by observing and interacting with the physical world and hearing the words that other people used to describe those observations or interactions.

Some of the words you learned didn't actually represent physical things, so how did you learn those? You learned some of them, like "the", "is", or "and", if you learned English, by imitating how other people talk, making correlations to infer when they should be used. And then there were some very mysterious words, called concepts or ideas, whose meaning could only be understood by a definition constructed out of other words. For instance, a "rule" is not a really a physical thing or process, but rather a concept that maybe have been introduced to you as something along the lines of "if you don't follow the rules, you don't get ice cream".

A dictionary is filled with words defined in terms of other words. Someone who knows the meanings of all the words in the definition can learn the meaning of a new idea or concept.
